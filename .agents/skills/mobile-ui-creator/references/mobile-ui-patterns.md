# Visual patterns from mobile games

Use the observations to make decisions about hierarchy, motion, and information density. Retain the target project's own art, labels, silhouettes, and game rules. The footage shows specific versions and the default timings in the handoff are project defaults.

| Game and evidence | Observation | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clash of Clans.** [Home Village UI article](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/weve-revamped-the-clash-of-clans-user-interface/) and [Build menu demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EeDTTzZU_o4&t=27s) | The village occupies the center. Primary entries are prominent at the edges, and four related categories sit inside one Build destination. Distinct icon silhouettes let the tabs stay compact. | Preserve space for the scene. Keep a small number of primary entries. Fold detailed controls into a context screen and give its categories a common surface. |
| **Royal Kingdom.** [Level tutorial at 1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tI1J3viSrDQ&t=86s) and [active board at 4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tI1J3viSrDQ&t=253s) | The portrait medallion, shared goals and moves strip, board, and booster shapes carry different visual weight. The tutorial dims the scene and points to the specific movable pieces. | Design silhouettes by role, combine related readouts, and animate contextual guidance around the active object. Retain a clearly readable play surface. |
| **Gardenscapes.** [Reward display at 0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CL0YD8wC71Q&t=2s) and [game board at 1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CL0YD8wC71Q&t=65s) | Reward objects and short amount ribbons read independently against a dimmed garden. The board is framed by compact groups for goals and boosters. | Make the reward art the focal point, keep amount labels live, use one backdrop when necessary, and protect the gameplay area from dense HUD chrome. |
| **Brawl Stars.** [Combat footage at 10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhvPlcwJUvk&t=600s) and [official preparation screen notes](https://supercell.com/brawlstars/en/blog/game-updates/release-notes-november-2026/) | Brief values appear at their combat event while health follows the fighter. The revised preparation view uses the map as a 3D backdrop. | Anchor transient feedback at its origin and keep persistent status where its owner can be found. Use actual terrain or world art to give a detail screen its focal point. |

Check each prominent surface at the intended device size. Try the same button with the icon alone, then icon plus label, then with a surface when it serves grouping, contrast, selection, or emphasis. Compare the result at rest and in motion. Keep selection, disabled state, and focus legible on a busy background. Reuse a shape for elements with the same meaning.
